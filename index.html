Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Evermore, EverAI, tasks, memberships and payouts.

How do I get paid and how often?

Eligible rewards from available training and remote tasks are processed according to the applicable payout schedule. Where three-times-weekly payouts apply, eligible rewards may be processed three times per week.

What kind of tasks will I perform?

Available tasks may include AI response rating, memory correction, opinion and survey feedback, transcription, AI content rating and other remote workflows. Task availability and compensation can vary.

Do I need prior experience to start?

Many entry-level tasks are designed to be straightforward and may not require previous professional experience. Specific requirements can depend on the task or training round.

What is EverAI?

EverAI is the AI-focused platform within the Evermore ecosystem. It is designed to provide intelligent assistant capabilities while incorporating human feedback and training workflows to improve AI interactions.
