Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Evermore packages and tasks.

What is the difference between Crown and Trial packages?

Crown ($10/N13,992 for 6 months) offers higher hourly task rates ($16.8/hr), priority remote jobs ($18/hr), unlimited EverAI code/media generation, and full academy access. Trial ($5/N6,991 for 2 months) offers $8.6/hr tasks, inconsistent remote jobs, and limited features.

How do I receive remote jobs?

Crown members get priority matching for outsourced AI jobs directly in their dashboard, while Trial members receive occasional notifications based on availability.

Can I build websites with EverAI?

Yes! Crown package members have full code-writing capabilities to generate and build complete single-page or multi-page websites directly through the platform.
